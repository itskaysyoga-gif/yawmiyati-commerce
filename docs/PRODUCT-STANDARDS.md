# Product standards

A product is published only when all gates pass and it scores 75+ of 100.

## Score
Contribution economics 30 · audience fit 20 · demonstrability 15 · spec confidence 15 · stock reliability 10 · competitive delivered price 10.
Evidence confidence is shown separately; a missing fact is never hidden inside a score.

## Gates (any failure = hold or reject)
- Exact product identity and contents verified.
- Media usable, with source recorded.
- Every material cost known (unknown never becomes zero).
- Positive contribution at 30% delivery.
- No claim that cannot be substantiated; no unverified third-party brand.
- Stock available and checked within 24 h.

## Contribution per submitted order
p × (S − C − V − R − W) − (1 − p) × F − O − A, shown at p = 30%, 45%, 60% until 50 own orders mature.

## Evidence labels
supplier-confirmed · screenshot-observed · researched-comparable · estimate · unknown
