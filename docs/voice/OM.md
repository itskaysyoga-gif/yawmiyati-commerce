Closed market. Shared neutral base: docs/voice/GULF.md. Evidence: docs/language/evidence/OM.md (thin: no Omani-owned
store with Arabic help pages was found; standard Arabic only; area-based delivery headings such as "داخل مسقط /
خارج مسقط" are a useful pattern).

## Before Oman opens: native review checklist
- [ ] Governorate and wilayat wording in the form and policy pages.
- [ ] Phone format (8 digits, 9 or 7 first) and its error.
- [ ] Prices in OMR with three decimals read naturally next to Arabic text.
- [ ] Brand lines: any local touch written by the reviewer, not by us.
