# Saudi Arabia (SA): glossary and review checklist

Closed market. Shared neutral base: docs/voice/GULF.md. Evidence: docs/language/evidence/SA.md (thin: international
retailers only, formal standard Arabic throughout).

## Glossary (what the Saudi store uses)
| Concept | Saudi store | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mobile number | رقم الجوال | الموبايل |
| region in the address | المنطقة | المحافظة، الإمارة |
| cash on delivery | الدفع عند الاستلام | كاش (fine in speech, not in policy) |
| delivery fee | رسوم التوصيل | أجور التوصيل |
| the customer (third person) | العميلة / العملاء | الزبون |

## Before Saudi Arabia opens: native review checklist
- [ ] Checkout labels and errors read naturally to a Saudi customer (phone format 05XXXXXXXX).
- [ ] Region names and the region word in the form, fee table and policy pages.
- [ ] Confirmation-call and payment lines: polite, clear, no timing promise unless verified.
- [ ] Decide whether any light Saudi touch belongs in brand lines (the reviewer writes it; we do not invent it).
- [ ] VAT wording, if any, after the VAT question is answered (launch checklist).
