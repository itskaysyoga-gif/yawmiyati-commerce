# Yawmiyati voice: Egypt (Egyptian Arabic)

Status: draft by Claude, 28 Oct 2026; revised 29 Oct after the language review. AI-reviewed only. Needs sign-off by a
native Egyptian reviewer (docs/LANGUAGE-REVIEW.md).

## Who we talk to
Women 25-44 across Egypt, mostly on a phone, often coming from an Instagram or Facebook post. Many order cash on
delivery for the first time from a store they don't know yet.

## Tone
- Warm and polite, like a helpful friend who runs a small shop. Never pushy, never cute.
- Light Egyptian dialect: everyday words, no slang, no street phrasing, no franco.
- Address her in the feminine second person: اطلبي، اختاري، تقدري.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Numbers in Western digits (399 ج.م) except inside the diary note.
- Say what happens and what she can do, not what is forbidden.

## Three layers (29 Oct 2026, review of the language round; evidence in docs/language/evidence/)
| Layer | What | How it is written |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Functional | buttons, labels, navigation, form errors, accessibility labels, category names | Short, familiar, close to standard Arabic, the same words local stores use ("الدفع عند الاستلام", "الأسئلة الشائعة", "إضافة إلى السلة"). Clarity first; dialect only where it is the plain word. Verbal nouns on buttons ("إضافة إلى السلة", "إرسال الطلب") so no gender is forced |
| 2. Brand and explanation | home lines, product benefits, diary note, tips, FAQ questions | Warm, light Egyptian Arabic; FAQ questions in her own voice. No forced slang, no repeated affectionate words |
| 3. Policies and money | payment, confirmation, shipping, returns, refunds, privacy, totals | Precise and unambiguous about WHEN things happen: nothing is paid before delivery; an order is a request until the call confirms it; a total is shown only when the delivery fee is known |

Button wording: the order button says "إرسال الطلب" (send the order), not "تأكيد الطلب": the order is confirmed
later by a call, and a button that says "confirm" next to a page that says "not confirmed yet" contradicts itself.

## Gender and agreement
- The brand speaks to a woman (اطلبي، تقدري، عندك/عندج) in explanatory text. Local retailers address customers in
  masculine or unmarked forms (evidence files); our feminine address is a deliberate brand choice, kept to prose.
- Products differ in gender and number (بطانية، رف، طقم دواسات). Shared strings never assume one: they speak of the
  product generically ("ليه هيعجبك"، "هل المقاس مناسب للمكان؟") instead of "ليه هتحبيها".

## Customer word
When a text has to name the customer in the third person, Egypt uses العملاء / خدمة العملاء (evidence: EG.md).

## Policy wording: soft but exact
| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| مش مسموح فتح الشحنة قبل الدفع | الدفع كاش للمندوب وقت الاستلام. بعد الدفع تقدري تفتحي الشحنة |
| ردّي عشان الطلب يطلع | ياريت تردّي علشان الطلب يتشحن |
| طلبك لسه مش متأكد | استلمنا طلبك، ولسه ما اتأكدش |
| وصلنا طلبك (reads as "we delivered" without the shadda) | استلمنا طلبك |
| من رقم غريب | ممكن المكالمة تيجي من رقم مش متسجل عندك |
| خلص من المخزن | مش متاح حاليًا |
| تقدري تطلبي لحد 3 قطع (plural breaks for other numbers) | أقصى كمية من المنتج ده في الطلب الواحد: 3. |
| اكتبي الاسم الأول والأخير. | من فضلك اكتبي الاسم الأول واسم العيلة. |

Errors: say what is missing and how to fix it, with من فضلك. No "خطأ" headlines, no blame.

## Words we use
دلوقتي · من فضلك · تقدري · هيوصلك · علشان · كده · حاجات البيت · الدفع عند الاستلام · رسوم التوصيل · المحافظة · أقرب علامة مميزة

## Words we avoid
- Orders and commands without a softener: ممنوع، لازم، مش مسموح.
- Hype and pressure: عرض لفترة محدودة، الكمية بتخلص، آخر قطعة، الأكثر مبيعًا (unless measured and true).
- Claims we cannot prove: أصلي 100%، ضمان، علاج، يحل المشكلة نهائي، أفضل منتج.
- The supplier's name. Customers only ever see Yawmiyati (يومياتي).
- Formal MSA for everyday lines (يرجى، نود إعلامكم). Plain MSA is fine for specs and policy facts.

## Facts
No promise about timing ("خلال 24 ساعة", "في دقيقة") unless it is verified on a real order; supplier
publications are "Taager said", not verified. Every number, size, material, capacity or time in copy must come from docs/COUNTRY-RULES.md or a verified product
record. Unknown stays unknown: leave the line out rather than guess.

## Diary note (ملاحظة من يومياتي)
One or two sentences, practical, in her voice: where the item goes, how to measure, one honest tip. No adjectives
about the product. Example: «الطويلة قدّام الحوض والصغيرة على باب الحمام.»
