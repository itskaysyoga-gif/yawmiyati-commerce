Scope: short labels only. Competitor fees, times and return windows were left out on purpose.

## What this suggests for us (Oman)
1. Evidence is thin and entirely international or franchise. Treat it as **interface conventions only**. No Omani dialect evidence was collected.
2. Use plain MSA for everything: "الدفع عند الاستلام", "التوصيل و الاستبدال", "مركز خدمة العملاء", "تتبع طلبك".
3. Delivery headings by area are a pattern worth copying: Muscat versus outside Muscat ("شحن مسقط" / "خارج مسقط"). We can add zone labels without stating competitors' fees.
4. FAQ questions in first-person MSA (هل يمكنني تتبع طلبي؟) are the norm here.
5. **Proofread carefully.** The Nespresso Oman typos show how sloppy Arabic undermines trust. Our Arabic needs a native review before launch.
6. Avoid English loanwords like "بيك اب". Use "الاستلام من…".
7. Some Oman grocery listings keep English product names. Ours should be Arabic-first, with brand or model in Latin script only where needed.
8. Masculine is the default address. No feminine forms were observed.
