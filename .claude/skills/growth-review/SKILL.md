# Growth review (custom skill, Yawmiyati)

Weekly, per country (docs/OPERATIONS.md):
1. `wp ym gates`: decision per product from mature orders with an 80 % band. Pause what the gate says; propose, never
   raise, budgets (money decisions are Rashid's).
2. `wp ym report` / ops board: unknown money stays unknown; record spend per SKU so contribution can be computed.
3. `wp ym reco-report --days=30`: items added from recommendations and their verified outcomes.
4. Content: platform IDs on studio records, results joined to orders only where consent and attribution exist; a
   missing match stays unknown. Platform ROAS is not profit.
5. Propose at most three tests for next week, each with its kill-gate and the evidence that would stop it.
