# Iraq competitor prices: summary (captured 2026-10-28)

Source rows: `competitors_irq.csv` (53 offers, 15 products). All prices are in IQD as shown on the page. No USD conversions were needed. "Taager" is the supplier cost / minimum selling price from `candidates_v1.csv`.

**Main finding.** At least four Iraqi COD stores resell the same Taager items under the exact Taager titles, sometimes with the Taager SKU visible on the page: Souq Waffar Iraq (souqwaffar.com/iraq), Bayt Asli (bayt-asli.com), Al Rafidain Shop (rafidaine.shop) and Talabtak (talabtak.com). So the main competition is other Taager affiliates selling the identical product. Souq Waffar puts a fake "50% off" strike-through on every item and sells at about 2.2–2.7x Taager cost with free shipping. Bayt Asli and Al Rafidain usually charge 15–30% less than Souq Waffar. Branded alternatives come from Elryan, Al-Hafidh and Iwan Center. Rahma (rahma-iq.com) and YOYOSO Iraq sell cheap generic versions.

**Sites I could not use.** Miswag search results render client-side and its product pages did not show up in search. OpenSooq Iraq listings returned no listing content or a fetch error. These sites refused to load (robots or errors), and I moved on: megamarki.shop, mihyar.shop, falcostore.com, mega-store84.almatjar.store, iraqdiscounts.com, ubuy.iq, hadaya4u.com and the Iwan Center search page. arafidain.shop is suspended, and konouze.online renders only "Loading…". I did not try Facebook or Instagram sellers.

**Caveats.** I read some prices from a store's search or listing page rather than the product page; the `contents` or `delivery_iqd` column says so on those rows. Al Rafidain prices in IQD and says it delivers to "all cities", but its contact number is Moroccan (+212).

## absorbent-mats (Taager cost 10,000 / min 12,000)
Only one standalone listing turned up: Bayt Asli sells the identical "طقم 2 قطعة موكيت فائق الامتصاص" for 22,500. Souq Waffar does not sell the mats alone. It uses them as an add-on in bundles at 29,499–33,999, so they look like a cheap bundle filler. YOYOSO sells a single small diatomite bath mat for 3,000. That shows how low a basic absorbent mat sells in Iraqi retail, but it is not the same product. Low / typical / high: 3,000 / ~22,500 for the same item / 33,999 for a bundle. Competition is moderate: at least two Taager resellers list it. The mat sizes and material are not stated anywhere, so check them before claiming "super-absorbent".

## double-clothes-rack (Taager 20,000 / 23,000): fewer than 3 offers
Two offers. Souq Waffar sells the identical item at 53,799: black metal, 150×110×40 cm, 35 kg load. Iwan Center sells a different double stand with shelves at 29,000, currently out of stock. Low / typical / high: 29,000 / ~41,000 / 53,799. Competition looks light. Souq Waffar's listed dimensions (40 cm "height") look like swapped labels, so confirm the real size, rail count and wheels with Taager.

## portable-clothes-dryer (Taager 25,500 / 25,500)
No reseller sells the exact Taager title. Generic portable dryers sell at 25,000 (Iwan Center, 600 W, 10 kg), 28,000 (Rahma, clothes and shoe dryer) and 28,500 (Rahma, with cover and timer). Low / typical / high: 25,000 / 28,000 / 28,500. That is barely above the Taager minimum of 25,500, which leaves almost no room at a normal markup. Competition is moderate. The Taager title promises a 6-month warranty, which none of these listings show. That is a possible differentiator if Taager honours it.

## foldable-extendable-mop (Taager 10,000 / 10,500)
Souq Waffar sells the identical mop (same SKU IQ030107MASS99) at 27,499, and also bundles it with the sink rack at 54,999. Elryan sells a branded Royalford telescopic flat mop for 10,250. Low / typical / high: 10,250 / ~27,500 for the same item / 54,999 for the bundle. A shopper can buy a branded flat mop at the Taager cost from Elryan, so the trend version needs a clear "folds flat / 360°" demo to justify about 25,000.

## wrinkle-release-spray (Taager 9,000 / 12,000): fewer than 3 offers

## lint-remover (Taager 11,000 / 13,000)
This product has the most competition. Souq Waffar sells the identical rechargeable 6-blade unit at 26,999. Generic or branded alternatives: Rahma 17,000 (USB-C rechargeable), Elryan Zilan 19,000 (battery or adapter), YOYOSO 11,000, Elryan Zilan AA model 8,000. Low / typical / high: 8,000 / 17,000 / 26,999. Lint removers sell in physical chains (YOYOSO, Elryan) at or below the Taager minimum, so a price above about 20,000 needs strong creative. Strong competition.

## steam-iron-4in1 (Taager 19,000 / 22,000)

## heater-180 (Taager 23,000 / 26,000)
