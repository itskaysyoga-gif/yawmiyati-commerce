# Egypt competitor prices: summary (captured 2026-10-28)

Data: `competitors_egy.csv` (92 offers across 15 products). All prices are the current selling price in EGP as shown on the page. Struck-through "was" prices are noted in `contents` but not used. Low / typical / high below use the offers marked same or similar. Offers marked "different" are reported separately.

## How the data was collected, and its limits

- **Amazon.eg:** blocked for fetching (robots disallowed), so no Amazon prices are included, even where search showed a matching listing. The spice rack, for example, is listed on Amazon.eg under Taager's exact name.
- **Noon Egypt:** product pages loaded without a price, and category pages returned server errors. No Noon prices are included.
- **Jumia Egypt:** the main marketplace source. Many Jumia prices were read from Jumia listing pages (marked "listing page" in `competitor`) rather than from each product page, because Jumia product pages often failed to load. Delivery is usually not shown on listing pages. Where a product page did load, door delivery was 25–30 EGP (free over 400–500 EGP), or 120 EGP for one heater seller.
- **Egyptian COD stores:** Dukkank, SouqWaffar, Rozks, BuzzTech, Kolshea, Makroma, Mozaik, Memo Shop, Ma3ana and others. Several show Taager's own SKU on the page (SouqWaffar, BuzzTech), so they are other Taager resellers selling the same stock. Most show a fake "50% off" strike price and claim free shipping.
- **Supplier images:** the Taager thumbnails could not be fetched, so item type was inferred from Taager's name and from competitor pages showing Taager's SKU.
- **Out of stock:** many COD pages show the item as out of stock. Their prices are still recorded, with "out of stock" noted in `contents`.

## Per product

**absorbent-mats** (Taager cost 180 / min 280): Only one store sells the identical 2-piece set (about 40×60 + 40×120 cm): Dukkank at 580 EGP, cash on delivery. The closest marketplace offers are Jumia's 2-piece chef mat set at 499 and Carrefour's 2-piece textile bath mat sets at 259 (all 7 colours). Single 40×60 diatom mats on Jumia cost 169–269. Range for comparable 2-piece sets is 259–580, typically about 500. Competition looks moderate: plenty of single mats, few 2-piece kitchen sets. Make clear in the listing that the price covers two pieces, so it is not compared with single mats under 200.

**double-clothes-rack** (445 / 610): Same-type double racks on wheels sell for 650–965 (Dukkank 650, SouqWaffar 729, Jumia 750–965). Telescopic chrome versions are 298–670, and both COD listings of that type were out of stock. Typical price is about 700. Competition is heavy: Jumia's "double rail clothing rack wheels" search alone returned 54 results. Sizes vary: Dukkank lists 42×96×143 cm and SouqWaffar 150×96×45 cm with a bottom shelf. Check which one Taager ships.

**portable-clothes-dryer** (600 / 900): Dukkank sells what looks like the same "large" model at 899 (600 W, up to 10 kg). Its page states the unit is **110 V and recommends a converter in Egypt**, which is a serious product risk if Taager ships the same unit. Similar dryers sell for 1,093 (Green 3ataba, 600 W UV, out of stock) and 1,250 (Dukkank, a smaller 110 V model, out of stock). A small hanging dryer is 295 but is a different product. Range is 899–1,250. Evidence is thin, and most listings are out of stock.

**foldable-extendable-mop** (320 / 340): This is a silicone floor squeegee with an extendable, foldable handle, not a mop (SouqWaffar shows Taager SKU EG030303MSSH99). Taager resellers list it at 524–574 with a fake 50% off. On Jumia, telescopic or adjustable silicone squeegees sell for **89–235**, typically about 120–200. Taager's cost of 320 is above every Jumia price found. Competition is heavy: the Jumia squeegee category has 173 listings. This is a red flag on price position.

**easy-squeeze-mop** (330 / 340): BuzzTech lists the exact Taager SKU (EG030107EASL99) at 750, but its page does not describe the mop head. On Jumia, self-wringing flat microfiber mops sell for 152–350 (most at 152–153; BIRDLINE at 350). Easy-squeeze sponge mops are 180 (Samara, an EasyOrders store) and 550 (BuzzTech, a different Taager SKU). Typical price is about 150–350. Taager's cost of 330 sits at the top of the marketplace range. Confirm the item type before using these comparables.

**spray-mop** (299 / 500): **This is not a floor spray mop.** SouqWaffar shows Taager SKU EG030107BAKK99 as «مساحة وبخاخة بلاستيكية 2 في 1 لتنظيف الزجاج والمرايا», a plastic squeegee with a spray bottle for glass and mirrors, listed at 549. Comparable spray glass squeegees sell for **65–261** (Hawaaeg 65, Milano Tool 70, Alhabeeb 104.99, Jumia Liao 160 and 261), typically about 100–160. Floor spray mops cost 280 (Jumia) to 632 (Open Dar) but are a different product. Taager's cost of 299 is above almost every comparable price. This is a red flag.

**heater-180** (565 / 595): Three COD stores sell the identical item at 749 (Rozks), 899 (Dukkank) and 919 (SouqWaffar). It is a small 20×10×10 cm heater with fan, wattage not disclosed. Branded or larger fan heaters on Jumia cost 850 plus 120 delivery (1,800 W, 180° outlet) and 999 (Comfy, 1,000/2,000 W). Range is 749–999, typically about 900. Shoppers comparing this small unit with full-size 2,000 W heaters at the same price is a risk, so the listing should state its real wattage and coverage.

**dish-drying-mat** (430 / 450): This is a 60×45 cm microfiber mat; one page describes it as mat plus PP rack.
